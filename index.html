About

A trainer who speaks both finance and AI.

For over eight years I have taught and trained across universities, regulatory academies and the banking sector. At the Bangladesh Academy for Securities Markets (BASM), the academic wing of BSEC, I trained CFOs, Independent Directors, Authorized Representatives of TREC holders, investors and university students. Today I serve as Assistant Professor and full-time trainer at the Bangladesh Institute of Bank Management (BIBM).

As Founder & CEO of Khan AI Lab, I build AI automation for financial data, research and education — so the tools I teach are tools I use every day. Participants leave my sessions with practical workflows they can apply the next morning, not just theory.
